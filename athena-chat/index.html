How AthenaChat works

A transparent, high-performance RAG pipeline.

1. Ingestion

Upload your knowledge base. We securely process your PDFs, docs, and text files instantly.

2. Semantic Chunking

Documents are split into overlapping blocks, ensuring no vital context is lost at the boundaries.

3. Embedding & Storage

Chunks are converted into dense and sparse vectors, securely stored in an isolated Vector DB.
